About

I am a pianist and composer. I started piano at age five with Claudine Boymond in Annecy, France and began composing at 13 with Sophie Lacaze and Ludovic Thirvaudey at the Geneva Conservatory. In 2019 I joined the Conservatoire of the 15th arrondissement of Paris, earning my CEM in piano in 2022 and continuing composition studies with Carlos de Castellarnau, and more recently with Jean Gobinet at the American School of Modern Music. My musical language lies at the crossroads of multiple influences, including European Romanticism, French modernism (Ravel, Fauré, Debussy, Lili Boulanger), as well as American musical theatre, jazz, and popular music. I compose for piano, chamber ensembles, string orchestra, and brass ensembles, aiming for music that is figurative, eclectic, and accessible. I am currently focusing more specifically on movies soundtracks.
